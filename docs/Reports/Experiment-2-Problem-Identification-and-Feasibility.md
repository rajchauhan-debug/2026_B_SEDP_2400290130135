# EXPERIMENT 2

## Problem Identification and Feasibility

---

## 1. Aim

To identify the problem addressed by the **CampusCareer – Student Placement & Internship Management System**, define its objectives and scope, and evaluate its technical and operational feasibility.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Students often have to search for placement and internship opportunities through different sources such as college notices, messages, emails, and external job platforms. Managing available opportunities and tracking applications can therefore become difficult.

**CampusCareer** provides a centralized system where students can view placement and internship opportunities, apply for them, and track their application status. Administrators can manage available opportunities and view student applications.

---

## 3. Objectives

The main objectives of CampusCareer are:

1. To provide student registration and login.
2. To allow students to view available placement and internship opportunities.
3. To allow students to apply for suitable opportunities.
4. To allow students to track their application status.
5. To allow administrators to add and delete opportunities.
6. To allow administrators to view student applications.
7. To provide a simple and user-friendly system for managing placement and internship activities.

---

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

The following features are included in the scope of the CampusCareer system:

- Student registration and login
- Student profile management
- Viewing placement opportunities
- Viewing internship opportunities
- Applying for opportunities
- Checking application status
- Admin login
- Adding opportunities
- Deleting opportunities
- Viewing student applications

### 4.2 Out of Scope

The following features are not included in the current scope of the system:

- Online examination
- Interview scheduling
- Payment processing
- AI-based job recommendations
- Complex analytics
- Direct communication or chat between students and companies

The scope is intentionally kept simple so that the project remains manageable and focused on its primary objectives.

---

## 5. Technical Feasibility

The technical feasibility of the CampusCareer system is evaluated based on the required technologies, hardware, software tools, database, security, and scalability.

| Area | Feasibility |
|---|---|
| **Technology** | HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB are suitable for developing the project. |
| **Hardware** | Standard computers or laptops with internet access are sufficient for development and use. |
| **Software Tools** | VS Code, Git, GitHub, and Postman can be used for development, version control, and testing. |
| **Database** | MongoDB can be used to store student, opportunity, and application data. |
| **Security** | Login authentication and basic access control can be implemented. |
| **Scalability** | The system can be extended with additional features in the future. |

### Technical Feasibility Result

The proposed technologies and tools are available and suitable for developing the CampusCareer system. Therefore, the project is **technically feasible**.

---

## 6. Operational Feasibility

The operational feasibility of CampusCareer is evaluated based on its usability and usefulness to students and administrators.

| Factor | Feasibility |
|---|---|
| **User Acceptance** | High |
| **Ease of Use** | High |
| **Availability** | High |
| **Maintainability** | High |
| **Project Benefit** | High |

### Analysis

CampusCareer provides a centralized way to manage placement and internship opportunities and applications. Students can access opportunities and application information through a simple interface, while administrators can manage opportunities and student applications.

Therefore, the system is **operationally feasible**.

---

## 7. Observation

Clearly defining the problem, objectives, scope, and feasibility provides a proper foundation for the development of the CampusCareer system.

---

## 8. Result

The problem statement, objectives, scope, technical feasibility, and operational feasibility of the **CampusCareer – Student Placement & Internship Management System** were successfully identified and documented.
